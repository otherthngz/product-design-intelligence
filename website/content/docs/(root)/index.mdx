---
title: What is PDI?
description: A practical guide to Product Design Intelligence.
---

Product Design Intelligence (PDI) is a collection of skills and guidance for AI assistants. It helps an assistant approach product design tasks with more context, from research and problem framing to prototypes and reviews.

PDI gives your assistant instructions to follow. It is not an AI model, and it does not replace your judgment or user research. Results depend on your assistant, its tools, and the context you provide.

## What you can do

- Ask your assistant to review a screen or user flow.
- Frame a product problem before choosing a solution.
- Explore interaction patterns, interface copy, or a design system.
- Create a prototype and explain the decisions behind it.

## Get started

1. [Install PDI](/docs/installation) into a project for an assistant you already use.
2. [Try your first task](/docs/first-task) with a clear goal and relevant context.
3. Review the result and ask your assistant what it checked and what remains uncertain.
