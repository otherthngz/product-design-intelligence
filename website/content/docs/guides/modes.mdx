---
title: Working modes
description: Choose how much collaboration, ceremony, and governance PDI applies to your workflow.
---

All three working modes are included when you install PDI. A mode adjusts **how much ceremony and collaboration structure** your AI assistant uses, without changing the underlying skills or granting extra permissions.

## The three modes

| Mode | Best for | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **`solo`** | Solo founders, indie builders, fast prototyping | High-leverage decisions, lean discovery, minimal ceremony, fast iteration. |
| **`team`** | Small product, design, and engineering teams | Surfaces cross-functional trade-offs, shared UI kit rules, and terminology consistency early. |
| **`enterprise`** | Multi-team orgs, regulated or high-risk domains | Emphasizes role-based permissions, migration constraints, accessibility/compliance governance, and traceable decision logs. |

## How to activate a mode

You do not need to memorize commands. Tell your assistant in plain language:
- *"Switch to solo mode"*
- *"Use team mode for this feature"*
- *"Evaluate this in enterprise mode"*

If you are not sure which mode or skill to pick, just describe your project and who uses it. Your assistant will explain the options and apply the right approach automatically.
