# PM request: onboarding feature specification

**Request:** “Make onboarding better.” Routed to `product-framing` then `interaction-design`; see [product outcomes](../../knowledge/product-design/outcomes.md). The absence of a product, users, existing evidence and constraints means the following is a proposal, not a verified requirements document.

**Problem hypothesis:** first-time users may not know the next meaningful step after entering the product. **User and business context:** unknown. **Proposed objective:** help a first-time user reach a defined first useful action; the product owner must define that action before an outcome can be measured. **Scope:** an initial guidance flow; authentication and billing are out of scope until clarified.

**Candidate requirements:** orient the user to the product; allow the user to start the relevant action; preserve progress where the actual system supports it; provide clear error and recovery states; work with keyboard and small screens. These are proposed acceptance criteria, subject to actual product constraints.

**Measurement proposal:** record the share of eligible new users who complete the agreed first action within a defined window, with a privacy-approved event definition and denominator. No baseline or target is known. Review drop-off and user feedback as context, not proof of cause.

**Questions for the PM:** Who is the first-time user and what is their first useful action? What evidence shows current friction? Which platforms, permissions, privacy rules, and deadlines apply? Who owns sign-off? **Next step:** gather existing research and observe a small set of real first-use sessions before choosing a flow. The [onboarding demo](../onboarding/README.md) is an illustrative artifact and does not answer these questions.
